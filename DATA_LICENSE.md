Project source code is licensed separately under Apache-2.0. These data terms

by users of the code.

## 2. Permitted research use

Subject to these terms, recipients receive a nonexclusive, royalty-free license
to download, copy, preprocess, and modify the released images for noncommercial
research, including model training, benchmarking, and evaluation of fingerprint
presentation attack detection (PAD), generation quality, and authorized
source-linkage and security testing.

Noncommercial research means research whose primary purpose is scientific
investigation rather than commercial advantage or monetary compensation. The
recipient's institutional status alone does not determine whether a use qualifies.
Commercial product development, commercial deployment, and paid data services
require separate written permission from the releasing authors.

## 3. Research reporting and attribution

Recipients may publish aggregate findings and a limited number of representative
images needed to explain their research in papers, theses, presentations, and
accompanying research materials, including those distributed by commercial
academic publishers. This permission covers illustrative reporting, rather than
distribution of a reusable image collection.

Publications and displayed examples must acknowledge this generated dataset,
cite its associated paper when bibliographic details are available, and provide
the repository URL and release version or access date. Identify material changes
to displayed images and retain supplied provenance and attribution notices.

## 4. Sharing and redistribution

Direct other researchers to the repository's current download location.
Redistribution, mirroring, sale, or sublicensing of the image collection, its
subsets, or modified image collections requires separate written permission
from the releasing authors. The illustrative reporting permitted in Section 3
is an exception to this requirement.

## 5. Responsible biometric research

Use source-linkable biometric information within the authorized research scope.
Recipients must not use the images to impersonate individuals, identify people
outside authorized research, or attack systems without permission from the
system owner. Controlled PAD attack evaluation and authorized source-linkage
research are permitted under Section 2. Applicable privacy rights and source-data
obligations continue to apply.

## 6. Permission requests and release records

Contact the corresponding author identified in the associated paper for
commercial use, redistribution, or other permissions beyond these terms.
Keep a copy of these terms with downloaded images and record the release version
or access date. Subsequent changes to the download location or terms do not by
themselves change the terms accompanying a previously obtained release.

These terms govern permissions granted by the releasing authors and preserve
uses independently permitted by applicable law.
